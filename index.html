About Abishek

Where craft meets
quiet obsession.

Eight years spent chasing light — as a photographer and a digital artist — has taught me one thing: the best work never feels designed. It feels inevitable. Click a card to read more.

01 Who I Am
A visual storyteller based between studio and street — equally at home behind a medium-format camera or inside a design file, building brand identities that photograph as well as they print.
02 Experience
8+ years shooting weddings, editorial, and brand campaigns across three countries, alongside 6 years designing identity systems and packaging.
03 Creative Philosophy
Restraint is a design decision. I chase the one true frame and the one right typeface rather than a hundred good-enough options — precision over volume, always.
04 Education
BFA in Visual Communication, followed by independent apprenticeship under documentary photographers.
05 Achievements
Featured in photography awards, recognised brand launches, and published editorial covers.
